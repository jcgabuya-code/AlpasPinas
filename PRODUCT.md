# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences share the site. **Prospective paddlers** — Filipinos (and the internationally curious) living in Malaysia who are athletic-curious, browsing on mobile, deciding whether to join a dragon boat team; their job is to feel the crew's energy and find the path to "join" or "train with us." **The existing AlpasPinas community** — current members and supporters who return for training sign-ups, rosters, events, and now to buy team gear. The shop visitor is usually already bought-in emotionally and wants an easy way to order and reserve kit (payment is arranged manually, not online).

## Product Purpose

AlpasPinas is the portal for a Filipino dragon boat team based in Malaysia. "Alpas" means *to break free / breakaway* — the name is the brief. The site exists to (1) recruit paddlers by conveying speed, synchronization, and belonging, and (2) serve the community with training booking, events, roster, and team merchandise. Success for the shop: a visitor immediately notices a featured/promo item, browses gear that feels unmistakably AlpasPinas, and submits a reserve/inquiry order with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality

Three words: **breakaway, synchronized, proudly-Filipino.** The voice is energetic and crew-first — confident without corporate polish, warm without being soft. Dragon boat is the one paddling sport driven by a drummer's beat that syncs the whole crew, so motion and cadence are core to the feeling. Emotional goals: speed and momentum (you want to move), tight unity (you want to belong), and a distinctly Filipino identity carried by the warm "sun" accent against deep-water dark/emerald.

## Anti-references

- **AI-template SaaS look** — cream/sand backgrounds, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section, identical icon-card grids, gradient text, hero-metric template. These are the saturated AI defaults; avoid them outright.
- **Loud, gamified deals site** — Shopee/Lazada-style visual noise, countdown timers everywhere, flashing discount badges, cluttered "add to cart" chrome. Promotions should feel curated and confident, not shouty.
- (From the design plan) the over-templated dark + single-acid-accent look and reflexive Bebas-Neue-only treatments; the warm `sun` accent is the deliberate un-templating move and is reserved for the cadence/promo motif.

## Design Principles

1. **Feel the cadence.** Motion and rhythm are part of the design, not decoration — the page should convey the crew's speed and synchronization (e.g. the stroke-cadence motif, wake underline). Reduced-motion users get a quiet equivalent.
2. **One deliberate accent, spent well.** Keep the dark/emerald brand equity; the warm `sun` accent earns attention only on the cadence/promo motif — never as a second general-purpose color.
3. **Promotion by curation, not noise.** The featured item grabs attention through hierarchy and placement (announcement bar + featured band), not flashing badges or timers.
4. **Proudly specific, never generic.** Every surface should read as *this* Filipino dragon boat crew in Malaysia — batik motifs, team photography, real cadence — not a sportswear or e-commerce template.
5. **Low-friction belonging.** Whether joining the team or reserving gear, the path to act is obvious and short; reserve-only checkout keeps the ask light.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**: body text ≥ 4.5:1 and large text ≥ 3:1 against the dark/emerald surfaces (watch muted text on tinted darks), full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and labelled controls. Every animation (cadence pulse, wake underline, reveals) must have a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` fallback — typically a crossfade or static state. Don't rely on color alone to signal promo/stock state; pair with text or icon.
